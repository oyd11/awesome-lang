# Kyrgyz (кыргыз тили)

Kyrgyz is a Turkic language (Kipchak branch) written in Cyrillic — 36 letters: the 33
Russian ones plus ң, ө and ү. It is a low-resource language, so materials are scattered;
everything below is free unless noted.

## Courses and lessons

* [Peace Corps Kyrgyz course (Live Lingua Project)](https://www.livelingua.com/project/peace-corps/Kyrgyz) — full course with audio, free PDF and MP3.
* [CeLCAR Kyrgyz language portal, Indiana University](https://celcar.indiana.edu/materials/language-portal/kyrgyz.html) — academic description of the language and teaching materials.
* [learn.kg](https://learn.kg/en) — a course from scratch built around 1000+ phrases with native-speaker audio, spaced repetition and drills; levels A1–A2 are published and higher ones are being added. Free phrasebook, phrase translator and level test need no signup.

## Phrases and phrasebooks

* [learn.kg phrasebook](https://learn.kg/razgovornik/en) — 337 real-life phrases grouped by situation, each with native audio and an English gloss.
* [learn.kg phrase translator](https://learn.kg/perevodchik/en) — search across the course phrase base (not machine translation).
* [Wikivoyage Kyrgyz phrasebook](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_phrasebook) — travel basics with transliteration.

## Dictionaries

* [tili.kg](http://tili.kg/dict/) — Kyrgyz explanatory and bilingual dictionaries.
* [tamgasoft.kg](http://tamgasoft.kg/dict/) — Kyrgyz–Russian dictionary.
* [Lexilogos: Kyrgyz dictionaries](https://www.lexilogos.com/english/kyrgyz_dictionary.htm) — collected dictionary links.

## Writing system and grammar

* [Omniglot: Kyrgyz language and alphabets](https://www.omniglot.com/writing/kirghiz.htm) — alphabet history (Arabic → Latin 1928–1940 → Cyrillic) and pronunciation.
* [A Handbook of Kyrgyz Grammar (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/A_Handbook_of_Kyrgyz_Grammar/Nouns) — cases, plurals, agglutination.

## Listening and media

* [Azattyk — RFE/RL Kyrgyz service](https://www.azattyk.org) — news site and radio in Kyrgyz.

## Exams

* [Kyrgyztest](https://kyrgyztest.gov.kg/) — the state Kyrgyz language assessment system, levels A1–C1, with official practice tests. A certificate is required by law for civil servants and, on a phased schedule, for teachers and medical staff.
