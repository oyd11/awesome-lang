## Television:

 * Hello Jadoo - Animation show - partly - an autobiography of a animator growing up in urban Korea in the 1980s. (avaliable with English or Japanese Subs; Also has Mandarin-Chinese and Malay dubbed versions)

## Web Tools:

 * [Tubelang](https://tubelang.com) - Search Korean expressions and see them in real YouTube clips by native speakers. Free.
 * [Mirinae](https://mirinae.io) - Korean sentence analyzer that breaks down grammar and vocabulary.
 * [Naver Dictionary](https://dict.naver.com) - The most comprehensive Korean-English dictionary.

## Courses:

 * [Talk to Me in Korean](https://talktomeinkorean.com) - Comprehensive grammar lessons, free tier available.
 * [How to Study Korean](https://www.howtostudykorean.com) - Free grammar lessons from beginner to advanced.
 * [Coursera: First Step Korean](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learn-korean) - Free university-level course from Yonsei University.
